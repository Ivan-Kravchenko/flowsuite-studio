No. These are digital spreadsheet products. Nothing is shipped by mail.

The products are designed with Google Sheets workflows in mind. Follow each product's included access instructions. Compatibility and behavior may vary by application.

You'll need compatible spreadsheet software for Excel files, or a Google account to create and use your own Google Sheets copy.
